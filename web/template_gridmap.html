traversability is scored against physical limits — 30° slope, 15 cm step, 10 cm roughness — the way grid_map's own filter chain does it, so ordinary road reads near 1. drivable is our calibrated verdict (1 drivable, 0.5 marginal, 0 non-drivable), whose 4 cm roughness threshold was swept against SemanticKITTI road labels for best F1. They are separate layers on purpose: a decision boundary tuned on one dataset is not a vehicle limit, and publishing one as the other paints good tarmac untraversable. obstacle_height is geometric — how far the tallest return in a cell stands above that cell's own ground — so unlike the detector it is not restricted to the forward field of view.
